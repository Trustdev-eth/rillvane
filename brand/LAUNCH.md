# Rillvane / RLVN

**Project:** Rillvane. **Proposed token name:** Rillvane. **Proposed ticker:** RLVN. Pronunciation: “rill-vayn.” A rill is a small current; a vane gives direction. The identity connects flowing liquidity with deliberate market navigation. The mark joins two channels into an R, with pale blue and cyan on navy in the terminal and a warm ivory landing headline.

This is a proposed identity, not a trademark, domain, social-handle or token-symbol clearance. Limited public searches did not establish an exclusive right to use the name. Confirm those checks before purchasing a domain, announcing the brand, or creating a token.

- `rillvane-token-1000.png`: 1000 × 1000 raster token image.

> Rillvane is building a Solana workspace for connected liquidity. RLVN is a community coin alongside the project. The current trading, supply, and credit experience is a simulation. RLVN does not represent equity, a deposit, guaranteed yield, or a claim on protocol revenue.

No supply, allocation, tax, vesting, revenue share, utility, launch time, mint address, or fundraising terms are represented as finalized. Do not market the coin as an audited protocol or imply live tokenized-equity markets.
